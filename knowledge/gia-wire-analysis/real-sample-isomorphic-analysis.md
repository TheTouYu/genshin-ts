### 真实 GIA 结论始于保存的样本与最小同构比较（A real-GIA conclusion starts from a preserved sample and a minimum isomorphic comparison）

记录样本路径/来源、大小与 SHA-256、精确命令、观察到的字段/拓扑、结论与范围；尽可能保留已知好/参照与失败/生成两侧。构造匹配接口顺序、类型、节点族与边界路由的最小同构 fixture，确认修复前不匹配，比较窄语义与 raw 路径，保留 focused 回归并分别报告编辑器/游戏证据。

Record sample path/provenance, size and SHA-256, exact command, observed field/topology, conclusion and scope; preserve a known-good/reference and failing/generated side when available. Build the smallest isomorphic fixture matching interface order, types, node family and boundary route, confirm the pre-fix mismatch, compare the narrow semantic and raw paths, then retain a focused regression and separately report editor/game evidence.

#### 适用边界

单个样本只证明其记录范围，不得提升为节点族或通用编辑器规则；查询/解码样本不授权写、注入、覆盖、删除或选择地图 ID。

One sample proves only its recorded scope and must not be promoted to a node family or universal editor rule. Querying or decoding a sample grants no permission to write, inject, overwrite, delete, or select map IDs. Revalidate when sample identity, command/tool version, schema, fixture shape, or claimed scope changes.
